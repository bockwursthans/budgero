Many households pay shared costs from personal accounts: one person pays the rent, the other the groceries, and both earn different amounts. **Reports → Household settlement** works out what everyone should have carried and who owes whom, without a spreadsheet on the side.

## Before you start

Everyone taking part needs their own login in the workspace. Invite them under **Settings → Workspaces** first (see [Sharing Budgets](/docs/sharing)). Each person then enters and manages their own transactions in their own accounts.

## Setting it up

Choose **Set up settlement** and fill in:

- **Participants** - members of the workspace, with the **personal accounts** each of them pays from and earns into. An account belongs to one person only.
- **Shared expense categories** - only spending in these categories is shared. Everything else stays personal.
- **Income categories** - income in these categories, arriving in someone's personal accounts, sets their share when splitting by income.
- **How shared expenses are split**:
  - **By income** - everyone carries the part that matches their part of the income in the period.
  - **Equal split** - everyone carries the same part.
  - **Fixed shares** - everyone carries a fixed percentage; the shares add up to 100 %.

## Consent

Nobody can be settled on terms they didn't accept. Terms take effect only when **every participant has agreed** from their own login - saving terms you take part in counts as your agreement. Until everyone has agreed, the period those terms cover is left out of the settlement.

Changing anything that matters (participants, accounts, categories, split or start month) needs everyone's agreement again. Each participant can withdraw their consent at any time.

The server only stores who agreed to which fingerprint of the terms. The terms themselves stay end-to-end encrypted with the rest of your budget.

## Reading the settlement

Pick a month or a custom range. For each participant you see:

- **Income** - eligible income in their personal accounts.
- **Share** - the part of the shared expenses they carry.
- **Paid** - shared expenses paid from their personal accounts (refunds count against it).
- **Fair share** - what they should have paid.
- **Balance** - paid minus fair share. Positive means money back, negative means they owe.

Open a participant to see the amounts per category. **To settle up** lists the payments that even it out. Record the payment itself as a normal [transfer](/docs/accounts) between your accounts.

## Changes over time

Use **Add change** when something changes - a new job, a different split. A change starts with a month; earlier months keep being settled with the terms before it.

## Good to know

- Only posted transactions count; scheduled recurring ones don't.
- Amounts are in the budget currency, including foreign-currency accounts.
- Split transactions count line by line.
- Expenses paid from accounts that belong to no participant (a joint account, say) are not part of the settlement.
